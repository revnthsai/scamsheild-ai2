    How the scoring works

    01

    Capture, on-device

    02

    Score against known patterns

    Decoded text is run through a transparent rules engine: link structure, brand impersonation, and payment or urgency language pulled from real scam scripts.

    You get a 0–100 risk score and the exact reasons behind it — never just a red flag with no explanation.
